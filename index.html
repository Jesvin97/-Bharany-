Look after your jar

Never use a wet spoon.

Water is the enemy of pickle. Even one wet spoon can bring mould into the whole jar. Use a clean, completely dry spoon every time.

  • Lid on tight

    Close it straight after use. Air and moisture shorten its life, especially in humid hostel rooms.

  • Keep it under oil

    The layer of oil on top protects the pickle. Press the pieces down so they stay covered.

  • Non-veg goes in the fridge

    Meat, fish and prawn pickles go in the fridge once opened. Veg pickles keep in a cool, dry cupboard.

  • Sending it abroad?

    Double-wrap it: tape the lid, seal it in a zip bag, then wrap it again to stop oil leaking in transit.

How long does a jar keep?

Our pickles are naturally preserved. The best-before date is printed on every jar. Once open, never use a wet spoon, keep the lid tight and refrigerate non-veg pickles. See jar care.

Can I keep it in a hostel room without a fridge?

Sealed jars are fine on a shelf. Once opened, veg pickles keep well in a cool, dry cupboard if you use a clean, dry spoon. Non-veg pickles need a fridge after opening, so if you don't have one, start with veg jars or a 250g size you'll finish quickly.
