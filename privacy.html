1. About this policy

This notice explains how Bharany handles your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Bharany is the "data fiduciary" for your order details. A Malayalam version is available on request.

2. What we collect, and why

  • Name, WhatsApp number, delivery address, PIN code: to confirm, deliver and support your order.
  • Email, landmark, order notes (optional): only if you give them, for the same purpose.
  • Phone number of the person receiving a parcel for someone abroad: so the courier can reach them. If you give us someone else's number, please let them know.

We don't ask for anything else, we don't use your details for advertising, and we never sell them. Your cart is kept only in your own browser and never reaches us until you place an order.

3. Who handles your details

  • WhatsApp (Meta): your order is sent to us and discussed over WhatsApp.
  • Google: if online order alerts are switched on, your order is stored in our private Google Sheet and passed through Google Apps Script.
  • CallMeBot: if order alerts are on, it forwards a short alert (order items, your first name and WhatsApp number, not your address) to our own WhatsApp.
  • Our courier: your name, address and phone, to deliver the parcel.
  • GitHub (hosts this website) and Google Fonts (serves its fonts) may see your IP address when you visit, as any website host does.

Some of these services store data outside India.

4. How long we keep it

order details are kept for as long as we need them to deliver your order and handle any problem, and then for as long as tax and accounting law requires. After that we delete them.

5. Your rights

you can ask us for a summary of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or update it, ask us to delete it (unless the law requires us to keep it), withdraw your consent for anything that relies on it, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 98460 20348 and we'll reply within 7 days.

6. Complaints

if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first using the contact above. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

if you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order for you.

8. Cookies

this website uses no tracking or advertising cookies. It saves your cart in your browser so it's still there when you come back.

9. If something goes wrong

if your data is ever exposed in a security incident, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Contact

WhatsApp +91 98460 20348 or message @bharany_kerala on Instagram.

Ordering, delivery and refunds are covered in our terms and conditions.
